Founder's Note

Why I built Ozyth.

English

I want to tell you why I made Ozyth.

A few years ago, my life was going through a rough patch. My exam scores were dropping, and I was stuck in a habit I couldn't seem to break. I tried a lot of things, watched YouTube, read articles, but everywhere I looked, the advice was different. Someone would say one thing was fine, someone else would say the exact opposite. The confusion just kept growing, and I never got a real answer.

Then I started reading spiritual texts and holy books, from every religion I could find. As my understanding grew, the habit I was stuck in fell away, and the confusion around my relationships and career started clearing up too.

A lot of people get stuck in the same loop I was in, different videos, different opinions, everyone saying something different. It wastes time and never actually gives you clarity.

I don't want you to lose the time I lost. That's why I built Ozyth, so that the understanding it took me years to find can reach you in a single conversation.
